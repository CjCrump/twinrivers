Custom Patios Built for How You Use Your Yard

Twin Rivers Concrete builds stamped concrete patios for homes around Brighton, Alton, Godfrey and the rest of the Greater St. Louis area. No two jobs are the same. Before any forms go down, Caleb walks the site with you and works out the shape, size, pattern and color so the patio fits the house and the way you plan to use it.

Stamped concrete gives you the look of stone, slate or wood plank without the gaps and weeds that come with pavers. We can tint the slab, add a contrasting border, or give part of it a broom finish for extra grip around a pool. Thinking about a carved seating wall or a fire pit later? We can plan for it now.

Every patio is formed, poured, stamped and sealed by our own crew. Caleb is on site for the work, so the person who quoted your job is the one making sure it gets done right.

Common Questions

Stamped Patios FAQ

How much does a stamped concrete patio cost?

It depends on the size, the pattern, how many colors you want and how much prep the yard needs. A broom-finish slab costs less than a multi-color stamped patio with borders. Caleb gives free on-site quotes, so you get a real number for your yard instead of a guess.

How long does stamped concrete last?

A well-built stamped patio can last for decades. Good base prep, proper joints and drainage do most of the work. Resealing every few years keeps the color rich and protects the surface from water and salt.

Is stamped concrete slippery around a pool?

A sealed surface can get slick when it's wet. For pool decks we can add a slip-resistant additive to the sealer, or use a textured or broom finish where people walk barefoot. We'll go over the options for your pool before we pour.

Do you build patios in my area?

We're based in Brighton, IL and work across the Greater St. Louis area, including Alton, Godfrey, Edwardsville, Wood River, Bethalto, Jerseyville, Collinsville and Granite City. If your town isn't listed, call or text and ask.
