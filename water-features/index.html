Waterfalls and Ponds Carved by Hand

A water feature changes how a yard sounds as much as how it looks. Twin Rivers Concrete builds custom waterfalls, ponds and fountains for homes in Brighton, Alton, Godfrey and the Greater St. Louis area. The rock is carved by hand, so it looks natural instead of molded.

Because the rock is carved concrete and not stacked boulders, we can shape a waterfall to almost any height and blend it into a retaining wall, a pool deck or a patio. Stone color is mixed to match your landscape, and lighting can make the falls the centerpiece after dark.

Water features are often part of a bigger project. We've tied them into fire pit patios, carved walls along driveways and full backyard builds. Caleb designs the flow with you so the sound and splash suit the space.

Common Questions

Water Features FAQ

How much upkeep does a water feature need?

Plan on topping off the water, clearing leaves, and cleaning the pump now and then. We'll walk you through the basics when the feature is finished.

Can a waterfall be added to a retaining wall or pool?

Yes. Carved concrete lets us build the waterfall into a wall or beside a pool so it all looks like one piece of rock.

Will it look fake?

Every rock face is carved by hand on site and colored in layers, so there's no repeating mold pattern. Look through our gallery and judge for yourself.

Do you build water features in my area?

We're based in Brighton, IL and work across the Greater St. Louis area, including Alton, Godfrey, Edwardsville, Wood River, Bethalto, Jerseyville, Collinsville and Granite City. If your town isn't listed, call or text and ask.
