How It Works

From First Call to Finished Job

  1. Site Visit & Quote

    We look at the old drive, the grade, the drainage and what will be parked on it.

  2. Tear-Out & Base

    Old concrete or asphalt comes out and the base is graded and compacted.

  3. Pour & Finish

    Forms are set, the slab is poured and finished, and control joints are cut.

  4. Cure

    We'll tell you when it's safe to walk on and when it's ready for cars and trucks.

Common Questions

Driveways FAQ

Should I repair or replace my driveway?

Small cracks can often be sealed. A driveway that's sinking, heaving or breaking into pieces usually needs to be replaced. Caleb will look at it and tell you honestly which one makes sense.

How long before I can drive on new concrete?

Foot traffic is usually fine within a day or two. Cars need longer, and heavy trucks longer still. Cure time depends on the weather, so we'll give you a specific date once the pour is done.

Can you add color or decorative borders?

Yes. Colored border bands, saw-cut patterns and stamped accents are popular ways to dress up a driveway without stamping the whole slab.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Brighton, IL and work across the Greater St. Louis area, including Alton, Godfrey, Edwardsville, Wood River, Bethalto, Jerseyville, Collinsville and Granite City. If your town isn't listed, call or text and ask.
